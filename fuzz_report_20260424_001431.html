How to Read This Report

What this shows

Each condition in your strategy (RSI check, price/MA cross, etc.) is tested across a range of slightly different parameter values — the "fuzz". If the condition only works at one very specific value, it may be overfit. If it works across a broad range, it's likely robust.

The charts

Heatmap (2D conditions): rows = indicator period, columns = threshold value. Each cell's colour shows win rate: red = low, green = high. A healthy condition shows a broad green plateau. A spike of green surrounded by red is a warning sign.

Line chart (1D conditions): Used for MA crosses and RSI-vs-RSI where there's no threshold to sweep — only the window length varies. A flat line = robust. A sharp peak = fragile.

Allocation tabs

When a condition gates multiple possible holdings, each gets its own tab. The sweep for each tab measures whether that specific asset outperforms BIL on days the condition fires.

Metrics

Win Rate
% of days the condition fired where the allocated asset returned more than BIL. >55% is meaningful; >65% is strong.
Profit Factor
Sum of gains ÷ sum of losses on signal days. >1.0 means the wins outweigh the losses in dollar terms. >1.5 is solid.
Trades (n)
How many days the condition fired. Low n (under ~20) means the win rate is unreliable — small samples swing wildly.
Score
Win Rate × log(n). Penalises high win rates that come from tiny samples. Useful for ranking conditions against each other.
vs TQQQ
% of signal days where the allocated asset beat TQQQ (your primary asset). Relevant when the condition is supposed to be an improvement over just holding TQQQ.

Fragility score (sidebar colours)

Robust
Win rate is very consistent across the sweep. High confidence the condition is real.
Stable
Minor variation. Probably fine; worth a quick look.
Moderate
Noticeable variation. The exact parameter values matter more than you'd like.
Fragile
Win rate spikes sharply at the fitted value. Consider widening the parameter or removing the condition.
Very Fragile
The condition appears heavily overfit. Treat with scepticism.

Comparing cells

Click any cell to pin it (blue border). Click a second cell to pin it (yellow border). The right panel shows both sets of stats plus a Δ row showing the difference.
